── §5.2 Update Paragraph (draft) ──
To assess coding reliability, we selected a stratified random sample of N=20 papers (36% of the corpus, proportionally allocated across the nine topic categories) and coded them independently using three LLM raters from distinct model families (NVIDIA Nemotron-Ultra-550B; Google Gemma-4-31B; Alibaba Qwen3-80B-A3B). Each LLM rater received only the coding instrument and the paper's abstract and evaluation methodology passage, with no access to the primary-rater codes. Four-way Krippendorff's α across the author and three LLM raters for the structural validity dimension (Q4) was α = 0.89. This meets the exploratory reliability threshold of α ≥ 0.67 adopted in the prescriptions of this paper, providing IRR-validated support for the 55-paper coding scheme and the 82% IRR misuse prevalence reported.
